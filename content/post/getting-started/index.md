## Overview

It has been over a year since I first arrived at HKU, filled with admiration and excitement. Now, my main focus is to graduate early without any delays.

Last New Year, I was working on a **SIGCOMM** project alongside my fellow third-year PhD students. However, in an unexpected turn of events, the senior member of our team quit in March, leading to the termination of our thesis. Several of my friends have also decided to quit. With no paper output and my supervisor pushing me to develop a land and air robot system, I found myself feeling overwhelmed. I was sceptical about the novelty of such a system, doubting it could reach the level of Mobicom or NSDI. Despite my resistance, I had no choice but to work on the project due to external pressures.

Without any prior experience or guidance in this area, I relied on my four years of undergraduate experience dealing with hardware. After six gruelling months, I managed to build an autonomous land and air robot system capable of flying, running, and navigating. I then submitted it to **ICRA 2024** and, along with my co-authors, submitted **5** additional papers to **ICRA** as well as other conferences like **CVPR** and **DAC**. I am also currently preparing for **RSS 2024**.

The only happy thing in 2023 is going to Hong Kong Disneyland with my girlfriend.

The year 2023 was filled with **pain and hardship**. Alone in Hong Kong, I had no one to share my struggles with. My hope for 2024 is that my papers will be successful, leading to a steady output of work, and ultimately allowing me to graduate in the second half of the year.

**❤️Happy New Year, Hello 2024**
