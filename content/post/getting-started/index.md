## Overview

It has been over a year since I first arrived at HKU, filled with admiration and excitement. Now, my main focus is to graduate early.

2023.01-2023.06, I managed to build an autonomous land and air robot system capable of flying, running, and navigating. I then submitted it to ICRA 2024 and, along with my co-authors, submitted 5 additional papers to ICRA as well as other conferences like CVPR and DAC. I am also currently preparing for RSS 2024.

The happy thing in 2023 is going to Hong Kong Disneyland with my girlfriend.

The year 2023 was filled with hardship. My hope for 2024 is that my papers will be successful, leading to a steady output of work, and ultimately allowing me to graduate in the second half of the year.

❤️**Happy New Year, Hello 2024!**
